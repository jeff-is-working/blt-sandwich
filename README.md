# What does a BLT cost?

A Reddit thread argued about whether $13.75 is a reasonable price for a BLT (the gouda BLT at Big Front Door in San Diego). So we checked.

**[See the map, chart and full data](https://jeff-is-working.github.io/blt-sandwich/)**

## Short answer

- **National median BLT: $12.00** (plain BLTs only: $11.00), from 650 local and regional restaurants across all 50 states.
- **California median: $14.25.** At $13.75, Big Front Door is $1.75 above the national median, and it is cheaper than about two-thirds of the California BLTs we found.
- The national price and the California comparison are moderate confidence. Big Front Door is the only San Diego restaurant in the sample.

## How the prices were collected

- All prices come from restaurants' own online menus and ordering pages, and were collected on **October 11, 2026**.
- One BLT per restaurant. Where a place lists a plain BLT and a fancier one, we used the plain one. BLTs with extras (cheese, avocado and so on) count.
- National chains, menus dated before 2025, bundle or combo prices, clubs, salads and burgers are excluded.
- 157 rows were re-checked against the live source page.

## Limits

- The sample only includes restaurants that post prices online, so it is not a random sample of every restaurant.
- A few states have small counts. Each state on the map shows its count and a confidence label.
- Prices change often. Some may have changed since October 11, 2026.
- Delivery-app fees are mostly hidden until checkout, so the delivery comparison is low confidence.

## Data

[`blt-prices.csv`](blt-prices.csv) lists every row with the state, city, restaurant, the item as listed, the price, and a link to the restaurant's own menu page.
